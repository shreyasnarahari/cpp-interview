# Templates & Metaprogramming — Learning Resources

---

### 1. learncpp.com — Function and Class Templates
https://www.learncpp.com/cpp-tutorial/function-templates/

Comprehensive beginner-friendly tutorial on templates with examples. Follow through the full template chapter.
**Time: 30–40 min**

---

### 2. C++ Templates: The Complete Guide (2nd Ed) — Vandevoorde, Josuttis, Gregor
Book — the definitive reference. Read Part I (Basics) and Part II (Templates in Depth).
For interview prep, focus on: Chapter 1–4 (function/class templates), Chapter 8 (compile-time programming), Chapter 15 (template argument deduction).
**Time: 2–3 hours for selected chapters**

---

### 3. Back to Basics: Templates — CppCon (Andreas Fertig)
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HqsEHG0QJXU

Clear CppCon talk covering template basics, specialization, SFINAE, and C++20 concepts. Great single-session overview.
**Time: 60 min (watch at 1.5x → 40 min)**

---

### 4. SFINAE and `enable_if` — cppreference + Foonathan
https://en.cppreference.com/w/cpp/language/sfinae
https://foonathan.net/2015/11/overload-resolution-1/

cppreference for the spec, Foonathan's blog for intuition on overload resolution and SFINAE mechanics.
**Time: 25 min**

---

### 5. C++20 Concepts — Rainer Grimm
https://www.modernescpp.com/index.php/c-20-concepts-the-details/

Practical guide to C++20 concepts with before/after comparisons to SFINAE. Shows how concepts simplify template constraints.
**Time: 20 min**

---

### 6. Fold Expressions (C++17) — Modernes C++
https://www.modernescpp.com/index.php/fold-expressions

Concise guide to fold expressions with all four fold forms (unary/binary, left/right). Essential for variadic template work.
**Time: 15 min**
