# Lambda Expressions — Learning Resources

---

### 1. learncpp.com — Lambdas
https://www.learncpp.com/cpp-tutorial/introduction-to-lambdas-anonymous-functions/

Clear, beginner-friendly introduction to lambda syntax, captures, and common usage patterns.
**Time: 25 min**

---

### 2. C++ Lambda Story — Bartłomiej Filipek
Book: concise, lambda-focused. Covers every evolution from C++11 to C++20.
Or blog: https://www.cppstories.com/2020/02/extra-cpp-lambda-story.html
**Time: 40 min**

---

### 3. Back to Basics: Lambdas — CppCon
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IgNUBw3YPNw

Comprehensive CppCon talk on lambda internals, captures, generic lambdas, and pitfalls.
**Time: 60 min (watch at 1.5x → 40 min)**

---

### 4. Effective Modern C++ — Items 31–34 (Scott Meyers)
Key items: avoid default capture modes, use init capture to move, prefer lambdas to std::bind.
**Time: 25 min**
